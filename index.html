Hermes

A private, single-user automation agent.

Hermes is a personal assistant application operated by one individual for their own private use. It connects to the owner's own Google account so the agent can work with their mail, calendar and files on their behalf — for example, to prepare a daily brief, schedule an event, or file a document.

What it does

  • Gmail — reads the owner's own mailbox to summarise and prioritise messages, and sends replies the owner has approved.
  • Google Calendar — reads the schedule and creates, updates or removes calendar events.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — searches the owner's own files and creates or edits documents and spreadsheets on request.
  • Google Contacts — looks up contact details, read-only.
  • Google Tasks — reads and maintains the owner's task lists.

Who uses it

Exactly one person: the account owner. The application is not distributed, not published to any app store, and has no other users. There is no sign-up and no multi-tenant service.

Where it runs

On a private server controlled by the owner. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
